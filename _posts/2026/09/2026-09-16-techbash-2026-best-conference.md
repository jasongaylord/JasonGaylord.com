We're now less than a month away from [TechBash 2026](https://jasong.us/tb). Our tagline from years ago is still applicable -- The best little conference you'll ever go to. But why is that important?

When visiting any conference, sure you'll find good content. But, the value of conferences grows beyond the sessions. It's meeting like minded folks. It's sharing stories from what problems you face and how you plan on solving them. As a smaller event, you get that at a level that's unique to small events. Our presenters are accessible. They'll be in the same lunchroom. They'll be in the same hallways. You may even see them in the water park or arcade. TechBash is different because our speakers want the conversation.

Hopefully we will see you there. If you cannot make it this year, we're disappointed.  But, begin planning for Fall 2027. We'll be announcing dates soon.
